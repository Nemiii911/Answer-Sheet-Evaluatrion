Q1. Photosynthesis is when green plants use sunlight and chlorophyll to make food.
They use carbon dioxide and water and give out oxygen.
Equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6O2.

Q2. Arteries take blood away from the heart and veins bring it back.
Arteries have thick elastic walls but veins have thin walls.
1. Arteries have high pressure.
2. Veins have valves so blood does not flow backward.
